Build Wall

One QR code per session. Attendees scan it, type what they are going to build today, and each idea lands live on the board you project. This page is for the presenters; the two links below are the ones to share.

Run of show

  1. Open the board for the session on the projector laptop and press Fullscreen (bottom-right, appears on hover). Press q to show the QR code full-screen.
  2. Click Presenter once and enter the admin key: it unlocks hiding a card (hover, ×), Export CSV and Reset wall.
  3. Ask the room to scan and answer. Each person can add up to 10 ideas and remove their own from their phone.
  4. Before the afternoon session, open the second board: the two sessions have separate walls and separate QR codes.
